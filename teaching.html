Teaching

In the 2026-2027 academic year, I am teaching MATH 1132Q: Calculus II as a UConn Mathematics Teaching Innovation Fellow. This fellowship allows a graduate student to teach a small (~50 students) class twice a week and two weekly discussion sections (25 students in each). My mentor for Calculus II is Professor David McArdle.

In Summers 2025 and 2026, I taught one class of MATH 1030Q: Elementary Discrete Mathematics for UConn's Center for Access and Postsecondary Success Summer Bridge Program. In Summer 2026, I was the coordinator of all the math instructors and tutors. Since Elementary Discrete Mathematics is a topics course, in Summer 2026 I taught the class as "Mathematics and Democracy" covering voting theory, weighted voting systems, apportionment, and gerrymandering.
